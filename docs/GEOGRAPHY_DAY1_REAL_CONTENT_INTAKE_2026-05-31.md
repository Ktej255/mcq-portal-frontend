## Recommended First Two Days

### Day 1

**Geographic Thinking and Map Relationships**

- Ask `what`, `where`, and `why`.
- Teach absolute and relative location.
- Introduce scale and map distortion.
- Explain physical-human interaction.
- Finish with India relationship drills from the map-intelligence PDF.

### Day 2

**Origin and Evolution of Earth**

- Big Bang as expansion of space.
- Galaxy, star, and planet formation.
- Accretion and differentiation.
- Evolution of lithosphere, atmosphere, and hydrosphere.
- Bridge into Earth interior for the next lesson.

### Day 3

**Interior of Earth and Plate Movement**

- Infer Earth layers from seismic evidence.
- Connect crust, mantle, core, lithosphere, and asthenosphere.
- Explain mantle behavior and plate movement.
- Compare divergent, convergent, and transform boundaries.
- Close with earthquake, volcano, mountain, ridge, and trench map patterns.

The Day 3 compact learner visual is now wired in Watch and reused inside optional Earth Layers review. It follows one evidence-to-hazard chain: seismic waves, layers, mantle behavior, boundaries, landforms, and hazards.
